1. OBJECTIVE
   - Overhaul the Pinaka web portal from a generic AI-templated aesthetic (neon purple
     glow, dark crypto vibes, narrow max-w-6xl centered containers with massive wasted
     horizontal space, and cookie-cutter 3-column rounded cards) into a dignified,
     institutional, and sovereign design system.
   - Ground the design in the reverence of its namesake: Pinaka (पिनाक) — the celestial,
     unwavering bow of Lord Shiva (Rudra), symbolizing uncompromising precision, discipline,
     and destruction of chaos/entropy.
   - Implement an editorial color spectrum: Deep ceremonial saffron (bhagwa/ochre), Kailash
     ice/slate blue-white, and granite bhasma gray/stone.
   - Eliminate cards in favor of an expansive, edge-to-edge Architectural Studio Ledger
     and Project Dossier system.

3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - High-trust institutional partners (university chancellors, municipal commissioners,
     and serious technical co-founders) immediately reject websites that look like generic,
     neon-purple AI templates.
   - The original centered max-w-6xl container left massive dead margins on modern displays,
     wasting screen real estate. Expanding to max-w-[1440px] with clear structural columns
     creates an expansive, magazine-grade authority.
   - Honoring the cultural and philosophical origin of Pinaka establishes authentic,
     sovereign brand identity that sets it apart from standard Silicon Valley clone studios.

4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
   - Alternative 1: Traditional SaaS Dark Mode with Neon Accents.
     * Why Rejected: Widely recognized as low-effort AI boilerplate; creates an impression
       of a casual crypto project or weekend hack rather than a serious venture institution.
   - Alternative 2: Maintaining the 3-column card layout with different colors.
     * Why Rejected: Cards inherently constrain information density and feel disjointed.
       A horizontal ledger format allows full technical specifications, highlights,
       and operational metrics to breathe across the display.
