3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - Aligns brand positioning with an open, secular, and globally respected product studio.
   - Broadens builder intake beyond a single hackathon, inviting any ambitious student
     or peer developer to incubate their software under Pinaka.
   - The diagonal blue-to-white-to-green gradient creates a fresh, luminous, high-energy
     feel while maintaining high text contrast and visual clarity.

4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
   - Alternative 1: Dark mode gradient with saturated neon blues and greens.
     * Why Rejected: Violates the user's explicit directive ("don't make colors very dark").
   - Alternative 2: Vertical linear gradient (top blue to bottom green).
     * Why Rejected: A diagonal 135-degree flow creates an organic transition passing through
       white at the center, giving the viewport dynamic balance.
