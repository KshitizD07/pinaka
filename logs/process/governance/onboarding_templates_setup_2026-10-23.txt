1. OBJECTIVE
   - Establish the standard templates/ directory referenced in Pinaka's core
     documentation (00-README.md and 08-lifecycle-gates.md).
   - Equip the Pinaka Studio with operational templates to evaluate new ideas
     (e.g., Smart India Hackathon prototypes, friend/peer collaborations), record
     architecture decisions, capture retrospective findings, and formalize
     IP and contributor agreements.

2. WORK PERFORMED & ARCHITECTURE DETAILS
   - Created the following templates under templates/:
     * problem-brief-template.md: Gate 1 deliverable focusing on evidence-based
       problem discovery, user access, and existing alternative friction.
     * opportunity-evaluation-template.md: Gate 2 deliverable with severity/frequency
       scorecard, engineering feasibility check, and security/privacy constraints.
     * adr-template.md: Architecture Decision Record for documenting technical
       tradeoffs and rejected alternatives.
     * product-case-study-template.md: Public showcase narrative for projects.
     * retrospective-template.md: Gate 5 deliverable for capturing reusable assets
       and lessons when projects are paused or open-sourced.
     * contributor-agreement-checklist.md: Clear IP ownership guarantees (creator
       retains 100% IP; Pinaka provides showcase/distribution rights), security
       commitments (no leaked secrets), and maintenance SLAs.
     * project-manifest-template.md: Standard pinaka.json schema definition for
       automatic or manual ingestion of child projects into the central registry.

3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - Without concrete templates, multi-product studios suffer from inconsistent
     evaluation, ambiguous IP ownership with external contributors, and a gradual
     accumulation of unmaintained prototypes.
   - Standardizing the intake pipeline allows rapid vetting of SIH / hackathon
     ideas and peer submissions while keeping studio quality gates intact.

4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
   - Alternative 1: Informal verbal agreements with external builders / friends.
     * Why Rejected: Leads to ambiguity regarding code ownership, branding rights,
       and long-term maintenance responsibilities.
   - Alternative 2: Centralized monorepo ownership where Pinaka takes full code IP.
     * Why Rejected: Discourages external builders from participating. Granting
       creators 100% IP while Pinaka handles the engineering baseline and showcase
       creates a mutually beneficial ecosystem.
